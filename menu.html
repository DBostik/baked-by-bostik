Cake Design Tiers

Tier 1: The Classic Beauty

Includes 1-2 cake colors, smooth or semi-naked finish, simple writing (name + age), and minimal décor like sprinkles.

Tier 2: Signature Decor

Custom color palette, textured buttercream borders (rosettes, waves), and light fondant accents (stars, clouds, shapes).

Tier 3: The Showstopper

3-4 layers, multiple hand-sculpted fondant figures (characters, animals, unicorns), and significant design time.

Cake Add-Ons:

  • Extra Cake Layer: +$15–$20
  • Fondant Figures/Animals: +$20–$50+ (based on complexity)
  • Rush Orders (less than 7 days): +$25 or more

Delectable Fillings

We believe what's on the inside counts just as much as the beautiful decorations on the outside. Customize your cake or cupcake layers!

  • Included: Our Signature Homemade Vanilla American Buttercream — smooth, creamy, and professional.
  • Premium (+$15): Homemade Chocolate Ganache (rich and velvety) or Homemade Strawberry Coulis (bright, fresh, real-fruit).
  • Custom Requests: We love a challenge! We are open to discussing Peanut Butter, Cookies & Cream, or other favorite fillings during your consultation.

Important Notes

  • Deposit: A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your date.
  • Notice: Order changes or rescheduling require at least 7 days (cookies) or 48 hours (cakes) notice.
  • Allergen Warning: Made in a home kitchen that handles dairy, eggs, wheat, and nuts.
  • Mixed Tiers: If you want a variety of detail in one order (like half simple cookies, half extravagant), we price at the average tier rate.
  • Fondant Work: Minimal fondant is included in Tier 2; heavy fondant work or 3D elements may push an order into Tier 3.
