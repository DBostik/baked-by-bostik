FAQ: What moves a cookie from one tier to a different tier?

You might wonder what moves a set from 'Simply Sweet' to a 'Masterpiece.' The price of your custom set is determined by the time and specialized techniques required to bring your vision to life. These 'extra-love' details require multiple drying stages, steady hands, and specialized tools:

  • Professional Airbrushing: Adds incredible depth and definition.
  • Hand-Lettering & Numbering: Personalized names, dates.
  • Metallic Accents: Hand-painted gold, silver, luster dust.
  • Texture & Dimension: Intricate basket weaves, quilted patterns.
  • Fondant Accents: 3D additions.
  • Detailed Florals: Multi-layered royal icing roses.

Cake Design Tiers

Tier 1: The Classic Beauty

Includes 1-2 cake colors, smooth or semi-naked finish, simple writing (name + age), and minimal décor like sprinkles.

Tier 2: Signature Decor

Custom color palette, textured buttercream borders (rosettes, waves), and light fondant accents (stars, clouds, shapes).

Tier 3: The Showstopper

3-4 layers, multiple hand-sculpted fondant figures (characters, animals, unicorns), and significant design time.

Cake Add-Ons:

  • Fondant Figures/Animals: +$20–$50+ (based on complexity)
  • Rush Orders (less than 7 days): +$25 or more
