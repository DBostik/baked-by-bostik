Baked By Bostik: Policies & ProceduresOur Policies and ProceduresUpon submitting your downpayment to Baked By Bostik, you agree to the following terms and conditions:ESTIMATE VALIDITY All pricing estimates are valid for 14 days from the date they are provided. After this period, prices may be subject to change based on ingredient costs and availability.ORDER MINIMUMS In the bustling world of bakeries and sweet treats, our order minimums (like our two dozen minimum for sugar cookies) might seem like a lot, but they’re rooted in both practicality and a commitment to quality. First, baking in bulk allows for better consistency—crucial for maintaining the high standards you expect! Second, minimum orders help manage the intricacies of kitchen logistics. Carefully prepping, baking, and decorating takes time and efficiency. Consolidating orders helps us streamline processes and reduce waste. Lastly, it helps cover the costs of top-tier ingredients, labor, and overhead. Baking is an art, and minimum orders ensure that Baked By Bostik can continue offering the best possible product at a fair price. So, while 24 sugar cookies (or a batch of cupcakes!) might seem like a lot, it’s a reflection of our dedication to delivering delicious, high-quality treats with every batch. If you need fewer, be sure to inquire about our seasonal boxed set options!PAYMENT DUE A 50% downpayment of the grand total is due upfront, with the remaining balance due at pickup. Payment is required so we can source the supplies necessary to create your masterpiece and reserve your spot on our busy calendar.PICKUP DATES & TIMES Pickup dates and times are set when you place your order and will be scheduled to accommodate both parties. A 30-minute buffer is allowed for pickups. If you anticipate being outside your scheduled pickup window or the 30-minute buffer, please contact us immediately. This helps us manage our schedule and avoid any inconvenience. Failure to notify us of changes to your pickup time may result in an additional fee due to scheduling conflicts. Thank you for understanding and helping us keep everything running smoothly!NO SHOW = NO SWEETS We believe our baked goods are best enjoyed fresh! However, we understand life can be crazy and we’re happy to work with you. If you need to reschedule your order pickup day and/or time, please reach out via email to BakedByBostik@gmail.com. Unfortunately, not showing up to the agreed-upon pickup time forfeits your order. Take heart, we’ll donate your treats to a worthy cause! (Please see our Rescheduling policy below for specific timelines).RESCHEDULING & COVID CARE We strive to accommodate our clients' needs and ensure their special occasions are celebrated with the perfect custom cake or set of cookies. We know you can’t always control when life happens or when you come in contact with COVID, but to help us provide the best service, please review our rescheduling policy:* Custom Cakes & Cupcakes: We require at least 48 hours notice prior to the original pickup date. Rescheduling is subject to availability. If we are unable to fulfill your new request due to scheduling conflicts, we will work with you to find a suitable alternative. A rescheduling fee of 50% may apply if the new pickup date is within 7 days of the original pickup date (this covers administrative and operational costs). If your new date requires adjustments to the design or flavors, we will discuss modifications with you. Custom cakes are typically non-refundable after production has started.* Custom Cookies: We can freeze your cookies for 2 weeks so you can stay home and rest, or reschedule your event! Please notify us at least 7 days prior to your pickup time.* General Note: Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to reschedule. The more notice we have, the better we can accommodate your needs. For rescheduling requests, please reach out to us at BakedByBostik@gmail.com.REFUNDS Your 50% deposit is non-refundable. Given the resources invested to plan, source, and initiate your order, we cannot accommodate refunds. In addition, each baked item is carefully and securely packaged prior to and through delivery/pickup. Therefore, we are not responsible for damaged goods after they have been picked up or delivered.DELIVERY We’re delighted to offer delivery services for our delectable cakes, cupcakes, and sugar cookies to make your experience as convenient as possible! Please note that delivery comes with a fee, which varies depending on the distance from our bakery to your location and the availability of our delivery slots. This fee helps cover the costs of ensuring your items arrive fresh and in perfect condition. To receive an accurate delivery quote, simply provide us with your address and we’ll let you know the cost based on your location and date.INCLEMENT WEATHER Living in the Midwest, inclement weather can impact travel and safety. If you are picking up your order from my home: please exercise caution and plan your travel time accordingly. My driveway is pitched and can be slick during winter months. Walk slowly and carefully back to your vehicle to prevent slips and falls. Allow extra time to reach my house safely, especially during snow, ice, or heavy rain.CHANGE CUT OFF Order changes can be made up until 7 days prior to the pickup date. Please email change requests to BakedByBostik@gmail.com. If you reach out less than a week before pickup, all change requests will be under consideration, and a small fee will apply.RUSH ORDERS We strive to accommodate every request with care and precision. While we understand that sometimes life calls for a sweet treat on short notice, we cannot guarantee the fulfillment of all last-minute orders. For orders placed with a turnaround time of less than 7 days, we will do our absolute best to meet your needs, though they will be subject to a rush fee. Rush fees vary based on how quickly you need your order, starting at a minimum of $20, and typically ranging from 25% to 50% of the grand total. This ensures we can prioritize your order amidst existing commitments without sacrificing quality.PRICE ADJUSTMENTS Once you submit the initial 50% downpayment, your price is locked! However, costs may not be exactly the same for future orders (and we’re sure you will be a repeat customer!). We always want to source the highest quality ingredients for the best possible product. Because ingredient cost dictates price, pricing for repeat orders may be different.INSPIRATION Thanks for sharing those inspirational pictures! They’re just that—inspiring. Your cookies, cupcakes, or cakes may not be perfect replicas, but we’ll try to keep it close!PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY We create replicas of client orders to photograph. If you do not want your cake or cookies in the spotlight, please let us know at the time you place your order.COPYCATS Copyrighted images are protected and we cannot identically replicate those baked goods. We can make a similar color palette though, so let’s talk!PICTURES ARE WORTH A THOUSAND WORDS … But they are not always accurate. Colors and lighting in photos may be slightly different than what you end up with. Also, cakes and cookies in photos may not be true to size.IT’S ALL YOURS NOW Once you pick up your order, what happens to your treats is all on you. Keep them safe and cool! Please strictly follow the instructions on your cake or cookie care sheet.SHIPPING (Cookies Only - Within Illinois) We do our best to package cookies for a potentially rough ride, but broken cookies are a possibility and a non-refundable risk you take when placing a shipped order. Also, we cannot guarantee that inclement weather won’t delay your order. Refunds cannot be offered, but know the cookies are on their way! Once tracking information is provided, claims for lost orders can be placed with USPS. (Please note: Custom cakes and delicate cupcakes are strictly pickup/delivery only).NO GHOSTS PLEASE We love Halloween, but we don’t like ghosting. To get your order just right, please reply to our messages within 24 hours; otherwise, we may just have to wing it. If you have questions for us, please give us 12-24 hours to send you a reply.COTTAGE FOOD LAW AND FOOD SAFETY Our products are made in a home kitchen not inspected by the FDA. Our kitchen is not nut-free, egg-free, dairy-free, or gluten-free.* Ingredients: We generally use a standard base recipe for our treats. Our cookie recipe typically contains flour, powdered sugar, butter, eggs, vanilla, and meringue powder. Cakes and cupcakes contain many of the same foundational ingredients but are subject to additions based on each specific order. Dyes used may include, but are not limited to, Red 40, Red 3, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1 & Blue 2.* Allergens: Please be aware that our baked goods contain dairy, eggs, and wheat. Our treats DO NOT contain nut products; however, they may come into contact with nuts or other allergens due to shared equipment and preparation areas. While we strive to prevent cross-contamination, our kitchen handles a variety of ingredients, and traces of allergens may be present.* Liability: You agree to notify your guests of this risk for any allergic reactions. We are not responsible for any allergic reactions to guests consuming the product. If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions, please inform us at the time of ordering. We will do our best to accommodate your needs but cannot guarantee complete allergen-free products. Your health and safety are important to us!Thank you for your understanding and cooperation!
