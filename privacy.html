Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

At Baked By Bostik, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use your personal information when you visit our website or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our order inquiry forms or contact us via email. This includes:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Event details (date, type, theme)
  • Delivery address (if applicable)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes.
  • Fulfill your custom orders for cakes, cookies, and cupcakes.
  • Communicate with you regarding your order status.
  • Send occasional seasonal updates (only if you opted in).

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data is used exclusively for internal business purposes to serve you better.

4. Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@bakedbybostik.com.
