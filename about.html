Meet Kristen

Welcome to Baked By Bostik! I'm Kristen, the baker and artist behind your custom creations. Based in Glen Ellyn, I specialize in designing cookies, cakes, and cupcakes that are as delicious as they are beautiful.

Whether it's a birthday, baby shower, or just because, I love turning your themes into edible art. Everything is custom-made with love and premium ingredients.

Pickup in Glen Ellyn, IL. Local delivery available for a fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

For cookies and cupcakes, the minimum order is 2 dozen. For cakes, the minimum size is an 8-inch cake.

How far in advance should I order?

I recommend booking as early as possible! Requests under 7 days may include a rush fee.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! Local delivery is available for an added fee based on your location. Alternatively, free pickup is available in Glen Ellyn.

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept Venmo and Zelle. Your date is officially reserved once the 50% deposit is paid.
